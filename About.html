Goal:

The goal of my website’s homepage is to pique the interest of visitors and prompt them to delve deeper into the pages of my website. The Art gallery online is a building or space for the exhibition of art, but in my case is my album of Artwork. An online art gallery is a website that displays artworks. Usually, the online gallery is run as a business, with the purpose of displaying the artwork being to promote it to potential buyers.
